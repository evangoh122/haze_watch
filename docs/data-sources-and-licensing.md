database table. Record: source, license, acquisition date, spatial resolution,
observation timestamps and vintage.

**Legal note (applies to all concession geometry):** public visibility through a GIS
service does not grant permission to download, redistribute or commercially reuse
Indonesian concession geometries. RSPO explicitly excludes Indonesian concession
boundaries from its downloadable shapefile package for this reason. Verify the terms
and legal status of each dataset before use in a public deployment (e.g. Hugging
Face Spaces). This repository stores such data under `data/` (gitignored) and the
public layer is anonymized + simplified (see `docs/pipeline.md`).

## Attribution and re-identification rules

- Public outputs must not expose exact concession coordinates, ownership records or
  polygon geometry that could allow reconstruction of real ownership.
- Public concession boundaries may be incomplete or outdated; surface vintage and
  attribution uncertainty in the UI and API.
- Geographic overlap between a hotspot and a boundary is **not** evidence of fire
  causation or culpability and must never be presented as such.
